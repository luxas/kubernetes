## What this commit does

Two review-driven simplifications:

1. Renames the predicate methods on `ConditionsAwareDecision`: `IsAllowed → IsAllow`, `IsDenied → IsDeny`. (`IsNoOpinion` is already Go-idiomatic and unchanged.) This aligns the method names with the `Allow`/`Deny`/`NoOpinion` variant nouns rather than the past-tense `Allowed`/`Denied` of the classic `Decision` enum.
2. Removes two APIs previously exported on `ConditionsAwareDecision`: `UnconditionalParts()` and `FailClosedDecision()`. Callers that need to bridge back to the classic `(Decision, string, error)` triple now do it inline (see the sample in `metrics_test.go:166–175`).

Also removes the invariant comment `INVARIANT: Exactly one of Is* must return true at all times.` that was incorrect (see review 01 finding).
